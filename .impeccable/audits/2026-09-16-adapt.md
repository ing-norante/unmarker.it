# Responsive adaptation

Scope: existing English and Simplified Chinese homepage and image workflow. The Image Workbench identity, colors, typography families, and processing behavior are preserved.

## Changes

- Result panels respond to their own available width. This avoids compressing multiple columns inside the already narrow right-hand desktop workspace.
- Previews split at 40rem of comparison width; analysis panels at 52rem; signal tiles at 30rem of panel width; verification cells at 48rem.
- Actions stack below 28rem of workspace width, wrap above that, and share a row with the filename at 52rem. Long button labels and metadata values wrap.
- Buttons have minimum 44px targets below 640px or on devices with any coarse pointer, including touch laptops. The slider has a 44px interaction height and thumb hit area, with edge clearance inside its card.
- The wordmark is capped relative to header width to retain its single-line form without overflowing.
- Preview frames allow loading descriptions to grow; images remain contained. Sticky upload positioning requires at least 700px of viewport height.
- The language suggestion respects the bottom safe area and can scroll within a short viewport.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove smooth scrolling and reduce animated transitions; textual progress remains visible.

## Validation and limits

35 existing localization and accessibility checks passed. Lint, TypeScript, production client/SSR builds, prerendering, and localization verification passed. Generated CSS contains the named container rules, wordmark size cap, touch targets, and motion preference rule.

The source detector reported the existing Geist font warning and documented typography-size advisories. These belong to the established design and were preserved.

No local browser QA session was launched, per project instructions. These checks do not establish visual fit or real touch behavior.

## Human QA matrix

Review both languages in light and dark themes:

1. 320px and 390px portrait: full wordmark, stacked actions, long filenames and metadata, all output states.
2. Phone landscape and tablet portrait: loading text, locale suggestion scrolling, touch slider endpoints.
3. 1024px and 1280px: outer desktop split with stacked inner results where space is limited.
4. 1920px and wider: previews and analysis use columns only when their containers have room.
5. 200% zoom: controls and messages remain accessible without horizontal page scrolling.
6. Touch laptop and keyboard: language/theme targets, slider adjustment, visible focus, download and retry actions.
7. Reduced motion: no smooth scroll, fade/slide, or repeated loading animation; text still identifies current work.
