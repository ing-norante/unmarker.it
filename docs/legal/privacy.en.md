## Retention

We keep data needed to provide the service and meet applicable obligations,
without creating additional archives for general purposes. The director also
manages retention through manual reviews and deletion.

| Data                                                                                    | Period or criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Images processed with the tool                                                          | Remain in the browser; no copy is kept on our server.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Purchases definitively abandoned or expired without payment                             | Deleted within 90 days of closure, after checking that no payment or dispute remains.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Operational copies of sponsor icons and materials                                       | Published until the campaign ends; copies no longer needed are removed within 90 days of its end. Only evidence needed for the contract or a dispute is retained.                                                                                                                 |
| Ordinary support correspondence unrelated to tax obligations or disputes                | Up to 12 months after the request is closed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Invoices and accounting documentation                                                   | 10 years from the last relevant entry, or longer if an investigation or law requires it.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Paid orders, accepted terms and essential evidence of performance, payments and refunds | Up to 10 years after the relationship ends for documentary obligations and protection of rights. A dispute may require retention until it is resolved. This does not automatically extend to every log or operational file.                                                       |
| Ordinary technical logs                                                                 | For diagnosis and security, within the infrastructure provider's available window. We do not create an additional permanent archive. Incident-related extracts are kept for the incident and deleted when no longer needed, unless required to protect rights.                    |
| PostHog analytics events                                                                | We use the Free plan, with an expected 12-month retention window. We do not create a separate analytics archive. At least annually, we review whether data are still needed and whether retention is applied, and use the provider's deletion tools for data no longer necessary. |

PostHog retention depends on the plan and how it is actually applied to the
project; the plan window alone does not guarantee automatic physical deletion
on a precise date. Deletion requests are handled separately. We do not use
session replay or keep session recordings.

Data that must be kept by law or for a dispute are limited to relevant
information. Deletion from operational systems is reflected in backups as
they are replaced under the provider's cycle; backups are not used for analytics
or to republish campaigns. Relevant deletions are reapplied after a restore.
The lifetime of a cookie differs from the retention of a server record or event.

## Rights and complaints

Where and to the extent provided by law, individuals may request access,
rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing
based on legitimate interests, by writing to the controller. Analytics consent
can be withdrawn through the preferences without contacting support.

Individuals may complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per
la protezione dei dati personali) or another competent supervisory authority.
Erasure does not remove documents that must be retained under a legal obligation.

NoMaDe does not use analytics for solely automated decisions with legal or
similarly significant effects on individuals. Stripe describes its payment
and anti-fraud checks in its own notice. For purchase problems, contact support.

## Cookies and analytics

The [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies) describes necessary tools and optional
analytics. Preferences can be reopened from the footer. Refusal and withdrawal
also apply to purchase-related server events for the same browser. The choice
lasts six months. Session replay is disabled both in the PostHog project and
in the client; we do not record sessions.

We do not initialise PostHog before consent. Measurements are pseudonymous,
not anonymous, and cover only visitors who consent. Local withdrawal takes
effect immediately; server-side withdrawal requires a connection and
confirmation. If it fails, the site shows a notice and allows another attempt.
See the Cookie Policy for the inventory of tools.
